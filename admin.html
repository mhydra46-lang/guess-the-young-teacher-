Commands:

/player/ /allow-replay/
Allows one additional game.

/player/ /exception/
Allows the player to play unlimited times.

/player/ /exception/ /remove/
Removes the unlimited-play permission.

/player/ /remove-score/
Removes the player's scores.

/player/ /status/
Checks the player's status.

/all/ /allow-replay/
Allows one additional game for everyone.

/all/ /exception/
Gives unlimited games to everyone.

/all/ /exception/ /remove/
Removes unlimited-play permission from everyone.

/all/ /remove-score/
Removes all scores.

/all/ /reset/
Resets all player statuses.
